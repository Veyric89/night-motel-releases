# Night Motel - release history

## 0.2.1 - 2026-10-05

The game is now called Stayover, with a new title screen and sign.
Story subtitles now say who is speaking, and whether it is a phone or the radio.
Each night opens with its number and a one-line recap of the night before.
The briefing shows the night desk's note, with your own notes from earlier nights.
Nights are called Night 1 to Night 5 everywhere.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-04

The first five nights now tell a story: an opening, a scene at the start and end of each night, and a new ending.
The game now updates itself from the title screen (Settings > Check for updates).
